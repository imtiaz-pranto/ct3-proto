Hook reference

Where to find your Pixel ID

  1. Open Meta Events Manager and choose your store's data source.
  2. Click Settings. The Pixel ID is the 15 or 16 digit number at the top.
  3. Paste it into the Pixel ID field. We check the format as you type.

Pause tracking on Meta?

Nothing is sent to Meta until you resume. Your Pixel ID and event choices stay saved. Your ads keep running, but Meta won't see new sales from this store while it's paused.
