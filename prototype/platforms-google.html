Hook reference

Finding the Ads conversion label

  1. In Google Ads, open Goals, then Conversions, then Summary.
  2. Open your Purchase conversion and choose Tag setup, then Use Google tag.
  3. The event snippet ends in AW-10938215/AbCdEfGhIj. The part after the slash is the label.
